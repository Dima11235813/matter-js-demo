# 7. Privacy notice ☐

**Why**: required before any research data is collected from players (Stage C) and before accounts go public (Epic 6 · Task 6.2.3).

**Steps**:
* ☐ Open [docs/privacy-notice.md](../../docs/privacy-notice.md) and fill the placeholders: `[contact email]`, `[name / entity]`.
* ☐ Confirm or change the retention periods (decision D8): raw research events 6 months, archive 24 months, inactive accounts 24 months, logs 30 days, backups 7 days.
* ☐ Decide whether it needs a lawyer's review (recommended before targeting schools or the EU at scale).
* ☐ Tell the agent when it's final: it then publishes it in the app (a link in the privacy panel) and bumps the research policy version.
