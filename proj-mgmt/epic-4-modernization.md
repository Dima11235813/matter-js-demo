# Epic 4: Modernization & Dependency Upgrades

## 📋 Overview
Replace outdated build processes (like Create React App scripts) with a ultra-fast build tool chain (Vite), update the core libraries (React 18+, MobX 6, Matter-js, p5) to their latest stable configurations, and enable strict TypeScript compile rules.

* [x] **Task 4.5.13** (2026-10-05, owner: "sometimes everything jumps off the screen and seems to be in the top left"): reproduced on a phone emulation by random finger drags (15 of 20 words NaN after ~50 drags, then Matter's collision detection threw every frame). Cause: Move mode froze every word under the pointer (`setStatic(true)` in a loop) but released only the last; grabbing a frozen word again saved its infinite static mass as its "original", and releasing it gave a moving body infinite mass, so gravity made Infinity × 0 = NaN, and the semantic forces spread it to every word (NaN draws at the top-left corner). The 14 px touch margin made overlapping hits common. Fix: grab only the nearest unfrozen word; a safety net removes and respawns any word whose position, velocity, or mass goes non-finite; molecule bonding no longer looks up members that are being dragged or gone. Stress probe (60 random drags, word adds, size changes) × 3: no NaN, no errors.
* [x] **Task 4.5.12** (2026-10-05, owner play-test on the live site: "the hand motion in 2D should allow me to move the elements around but … the tap interaction is not registering on my mobile device"): p5 1.x never turns a touchstart into mousePressed, so a finger never grabbed a word (taps only worked through emulated mouse events). Touch handlers now drive the same press/drag/release; the canvas cancels emulated mouse events and the browser's pan/zoom (`touch-action: none`); fingers get a 14 px hit margin; a held word stays on the board (dragged past the edge, it was deleted as out of bounds, found by the phone e2e). Dashboard: tap the header to dock it as a small chip in the board's bottom-right corner, tap the chip to restore (owner: "tap to collapse it so it takes up minimal space and docks somewhere").
